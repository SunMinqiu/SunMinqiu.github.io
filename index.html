Biography 简介

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer & Information Sciences at the University of Delaware, advised by Prof. Dong Dai. My research focuses on Systems for AI and Large Language Models. My work so far centers on efficient checkpointing and fault tolerance for LLM post-training. I am currently exploring system support for scientific workflows and LLM agent provenance.
